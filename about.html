Our Family Kitchen

I love to cook and bake, and I have been doing so since I was a child. My family has always been passionate about food, and we love to share our favorite recipes with friends and family.

Recipes Made to Share

Our name "Ohana Bento" contains both a Hawaiian and Japanese word. Ohana translates to "family", and Bento translates to "boxed meal". Our recipes are made to be shared with loved ones. We believe that food brings people together, and we hope that our recipes inspire you to create delicious meals for your own family and friends.
